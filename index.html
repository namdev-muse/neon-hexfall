HOW TO PLAY

Hex trios fall from the top. Match 3 or more connected hexes of the same colour to clear them.

Chains multiply your score — clears that cascade after a fall count double, triple, and more. Big chains flash a CHAIN ×N banner. Matches within 5 seconds of each other also build a COMBO multiplier, up to double points — keep clearing to keep it alive.

Bigger matches pay more — 4 hexes earn a bonus, 5+ earn a big one. Clear the whole board for a huge ALL CLEAR bonus.

Watch for special hexes dropping inside trios: a 💣 bomb hex detonates when a match clears next to it, blasting the hexes around it (and setting off nearby bombs). A 🌈 rainbow hex matches any colour. A ⏳ slow-tide hex freezes the rising tide for 15 seconds when a match clears next to it, and a ✂ tide-break hex clears the whole bottom two rows the same way. A ❄ freeze hex is different: matching next to it banks it instead of firing — hold up to 3, then press F (or the ❄ button) to freeze the tide for 10 seconds exactly when you need it.

From level 2, elite hexes drop in trios too: a 🛡 armored hex takes two hits to clear — the first match only cracks it. A 🔒 locked hex can't match at all until a clear next to it breaks the lock. A ★ star hex pays a +250 bonus when it clears. Locks start appearing at level 3.

The TIDE rises as you play. When it fills, a row of grey slag surges up from the bottom. Slag never matches — clear it by matching next to it. At higher levels the tide can surge twice in a row, and slag may rise in patterns — rows with gaps, or columns of slag. When the tide is in its last 20%, the waterline pulses red and a warning tick sounds.

If the stack reaches the top, you lose a life and the tide breaks the top rows away. Lose all 3 lives and the run ends.

Everyone plays the same trios each day — beat the daily best for your seed and climb the ranks from Rookie to Hex Legend. Share your seed link from the menu to challenge a friend to the same board.

Modes: CLASSIC is the 3-lives run with the rising tide. ⚡ BLITZ 60s gives you one minute to score as much as you can — the clock replaces the lives counter. ENDLESS has no lives: a single top-out ends the run. ZEN has no tide and no lives lost, so you can play as long as you like. Each mode keeps its own best score.

Audio: the AUDIO panel on the menu toggles music and sound effects separately, picks a music style — PULSE, DRIFT or DRIVE — and sets the volume. The countdown under the tide bar tells you how long until the next surge.
